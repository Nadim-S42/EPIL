About EPIL

Researching pathogens to address important health challenges

The Enteric Pathogens Intelligence Lab focuses on understanding infectious pathogens and their impacts on human health. We bring together laboratory investigation, epidemiological evidence, and analytical approaches to study disease patterns and pathogen biology.

Our interests include enteric viruses, emerging infectious diseases, molecular epidemiology, host-pathogen interactions, and the development and evaluation of laboratory assays.

Our research approach

We aim to connect molecular findings with epidemiological evidence to improve understanding of pathogen transmission, disease burden, immune responses, and opportunities for improved surveillance and prevention.

What We Study

Research Areas

Our research spans pathogen biology, disease surveillance, immune responses, and computational analysis.

⌬

Enteric Pathogens

Investigating enteric viruses and other gastrointestinal pathogens associated with infectious disease and diarrhea.

⌘

Molecular Epidemiology

Examining pathogen diversity, genetic variation, evolutionary patterns, and the epidemiology of emerging infections.

◎

Immunology and Assays

Studying antibody responses, neutralization, serological assays, and laboratory approaches to characterize immunity.

⌁

Infectious Disease Intelligence

Applying epidemiological and computational analyses to understand disease trends and support evidence-based research.
