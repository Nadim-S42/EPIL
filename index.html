Our Focus

Research Areas

Exploring enteric pathogens through microbiology, molecular investigation, and data-informed public health research.

◉

Enteric Viral Pathogens

Investigating rotavirus and other enteric viruses, their biology, epidemiology, and impact on human health.

⌁

Bacterial Pathogens

Studying bacterial enteric pathogens, including Vibrio cholerae, and questions relevant to disease prevention and control.

⚗

Antimicrobial Research

Exploring antimicrobial mechanisms, resistance, and molecular approaches to understanding pathogen response.

⌬

Molecular Microbiology

Using molecular and laboratory methods to investigate pathogen characteristics and host–pathogen interactions.

▤

Genomic & Data Analysis

Applying data-driven approaches to interpret pathogen patterns, genomic information, and infectious disease trends.

◎

Global Health

Connecting laboratory research with questions that matter for enteric disease surveillance and public health.

Who We Are

Research with a public-health purpose

The Enteric Pathogen Intelligence Lab is based in the Department of Microbiology at Jahangirnagar University. The lab’s website brings together its research themes, team, publications, and opportunities for scientific collaboration.

Our focus is understanding enteric pathogens and supporting evidence-informed approaches to infectious disease research.

Our approach
Rigorous science · Collaborative research · Responsible data use · Public-health relevance
